Mbappe lives in your menu bar and gives you instant visibility into every local service running on your Mac — databases, servers, daemons — all in one place.

Inspired by JetBrains' Services panel, Mbappe lets you start, stop, and restart services without ever touching a terminal.

Features:
• Live status for all your local services
• One-click start / stop / restart
• Port and PID at a glance
• Minimal, focused UI — no clutter
